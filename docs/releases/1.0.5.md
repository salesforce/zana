# What's new in 1.0.5

A big release for teams and squads — visualize your squad, edit teams in the app, answer agents from Slack — plus a friendlier terminal and release notes on every update.

## ✨ New

- **See your squad as a live flow.** A new Squad Flow view visualizes your team as a diagram — who's working, how work hands off — with a sticky, project-labelled switcher to move between squads. Autonomous runs get a supervisor that keeps them on track.
- **Edit your teams right in the app.** Build and tweak team templates and profiles from the Teams tab — no more hand-editing files. Comes with ready-to-use "core dev squad" personas and a team to start from.
- **Answer agents from Slack.** When an agent goes idle or needs you, it can post a summary into your Slack thread and relay your reply back — so you can keep things moving without leaving Slack.
- **Know what's new after every update.** The app shows a friendly summary of what changed the first time you open it after updating — and you can revisit it any time from Settings → About.
- **Make the terminal yours.** Pick your own color theme for the terminal, independent of the rest of the app, with a reorganized Settings screen that's easier to navigate.
- **Support the project.** A new ♥ Star affordance in the footer makes it easy to show your support.

## 💬 Improved

- **Simpler launching.** The separate "Squad" picker is gone — there's now one clear vocabulary for starting work in each context. The redundant global Zana sidebar entry was removed too.
- **Remote teams reconnect after sleep.** Remote sessions re-establish themselves cleanly when your machine wakes up.
- **Friendlier model names.** You can refer to models by simple aliases and the app resolves them for you. You can also opt out of scroll-wheel scrolling in the terminal.
- **A self-theming terminal.** Terminal colors follow the app's light or dark appearance automatically.
- **Questions never dead-end.** When an agent asks you something, you can always answer it — and the options an agent offers now carry through to the question you're asked.
- **A steadier inbox.** The section you're reading stays put instead of collapsing on you, and selected rows no longer balloon in size.
- **Smoother first run.** The installer now sets up your workspace for you.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Scheduler.** The cron scheduling controls are back, and per-row actions work in the "All schedules" view.
- Removed a stray, non-working "Open in workspace" button from the agent view.

---

_Some capabilities (like Slack answer-relay) are opt-in — enable them in Settings when you're ready._
