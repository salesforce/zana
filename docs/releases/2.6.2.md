# What's new in 2.6.2

**Required role is now a hard rule in every Squad, and plan intake forgives formatting slips.** A step that names a role no member offers is caught before launch, and a step that no worker can take escalates to a person instead of failing the run.

## Squads

- **Required role is enforced in every Squad.** A step with `Required role` now runs only on a member that answers to every listed role. If no member does, launch is blocked and the check names the step, for example `work unit build has no worker slot that qualifies`, with a fix such as `Use "Architect" as the required role.` or `Change the required role to one this Squad offers: Architect, Reviewer.` Change the role or add a member, then launch again.
- **Runs already in flight keep their old dispatch.** Only launches made after the update use the new rule.
- **An approved offer is checked again at Launch.** A Squad offer you approved but have not launched is re-checked when you press Launch, so a Squad edited in between can no longer start a plan it cannot run.
- **Plan intake tolerates formatting deviations.** A plan with different label capitalization, extra spacing or backticks, or similar slips is read as intended, and anything that still cannot be read appears in Review plan with a suggested correction instead of a bare error.
- **Steps no worker can take now escalate instead of failing.** The step waits behind a coordinator blocker, then a human Inbox blocker once the coordinator is gone. Your reply ends the step cleanly, and it never runs on a member that does not match. When a worker's model level is unknown, the engine waits a bounded time for it before escalating.
- **Plan correction and Review plan.** The Squad launch composer and the Inbox card show the plan check, with one-click corrections where the fix is exact.

- **A plan with a Must fix now asks you what to do first.** When an agent prepares a launch for a plan that cannot run, it asks which fix to use (change the role, drop the requirement, or prepare it as it is) instead of silently preparing a launch that cannot start.
- **Fix a blocked launch from the composer.** When Review plan shows a Must fix, a "How to fix it" section lets you copy a fix request, send it straight to the conversation that prepared the launch, or re-check the plan after you edit it. Launch turns on once nothing is left to fix. The prompt box is now labelled "Squad goal", so it is clear it goes to the Squad, not to the agent.
- **Plan notes stay out of sight.** Internal markers such as `<!-- executable-step -->` no longer show in the Inbox plan view.

## Compatibility

- **Older Zana versions ignore the new routing fields.** After a downgrade, the new routing fields in a plan are skipped and steps dispatch as before; `Required role` is then no longer a hard rule.
