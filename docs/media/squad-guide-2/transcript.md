# Squad guide video 2: transcript

Before you start, you need a Project open in Zana and one signed-in agent tool. Claude is the recommended choice.

Click New agent, and under Launch mode, choose Squad.

Pick Research Squad. It has two Low-tier investigators, a good fit for comparing options.

Leave Squad planning on Infer plan from goal. You describe what you want, and the Orchestrator writes the plan.

Describe the goal in plain words: what to look at, and what the report should contain. Be specific, and name the files or areas that matter.

Goals hold up to 2,048 characters. Over that, Launch is disabled, so attach a plan or source instead.

Check the Project, add a Title if you like, then press Launch squad.

Zana checks the Squad's requirements first. If something is missing, it says what, and no workers start.

A run takes as long as the plan needs.
