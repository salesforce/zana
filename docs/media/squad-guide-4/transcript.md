# Squad guide video 4: transcript

These are starting points for common jobs, so you can begin without building anything.

The Debug Squad is for finding the cause of a failure.

The Delivery Squad is for making a change that gets reviewed.

The Design Squad is for a technical design that gets challenged.

The Research Squad is for comparing options or surveying code.

The Review Squad is for a focused review of a diff or branch.

Use them as they are. Once you understand Squads, edit them or make your own.
