# Squad guide video 4: labels

- Built-in Squads: These are starting points for common jobs, so you can begin without building anything.
- Debug: find the cause of a failure: The Debug Squad is for finding the cause of a failure.
- Delivery: a change, built and reviewed: The Delivery Squad is for making a change that gets reviewed.
- Design: a design that gets challenged: The Design Squad is for a technical design that gets challenged.
- Research: compare options, survey code: The Research Squad is for comparing options or surveying code.
- Review: a focused review of a diff: The Review Squad is for a focused review of a diff or branch.
- Use them, edit them, or make your own: Use them as they are. Once you understand Squads, edit them or make your own.
