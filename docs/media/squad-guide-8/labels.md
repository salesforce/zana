# Squad guide video 8: labels

- Use a Squad to accomplish a Goal: A Goal is an aim the app keeps working toward, one iteration at a time, until it is met or the app gives up. Goals are off by default. Turn them on in Settings, Experimental, Goals. Then open a Project's Goals tab and press New goal.
- Run as Squad: Set Run as to Squad, and write the plan or prompt. Then choose the Squad, the Project, and Squad planning.
- Max iterations and No-progress limit: Max iterations is a hard ceiling on how many times the Goal runs. The no-progress limit is how many stalled rounds pass before it escalates to you.
- Each iteration is a Squad job: Each iteration runs as a durable Squad job. After each one, the app judges the result and decides whether to go again.
- The outcome lands in your Inbox: When the Goal ends, its outcome appears in your Inbox, titled Goal, followed by its name.
- A Goal pursues an aim: A Goal keeps going until the aim is met.
