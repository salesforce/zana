Give a Squad an aim.

Judged after every iteration.

The outcome lands in your Inbox.

A Goal is an aim the app keeps working toward, one iteration at a time, until it is met or the app gives up. Goals are off by default. Turn them on in Settings, Experimental, Goals. Then open a Project's Goals tab and press New goal.

Set Run as to Squad, and write the plan or prompt. Then choose the Squad, the Project, and Squad planning.

Max iterations is a hard ceiling on how many times the Goal runs. The no-progress limit is how many stalled rounds pass before it escalates to you.

Each iteration runs as a durable Squad job. After each one, the app judges the result and decides whether to go again.

When the Goal ends, its outcome appears in your Inbox, titled Goal, followed by its name.

A Goal keeps going until the aim is met.
