Meet Zana for Slack, shown here with Pi.

In a linked channel, just mention Zana with your task. Zana uses that channel’s Project and harness: Pi here, or any harness installed in Zana.

Pi works on your own computer. When it needs a decision, it asks right in the Slack thread. Answer there, and Pi fixes the code and reruns the tests.

When the turn ends, Zana posts the answer right back in your Slack thread: what changed, and how the tests did.

Reply in the thread to keep going. Zana continues the same conversation, with the same Project, machine, and harness.

Or message Zana privately. Ask for your unread reports, and it reads them straight from your Zana inbox.

Zana Home lists every agent, so you can follow, mute, or stop them. Just keep your computer awake, with Zana running.
