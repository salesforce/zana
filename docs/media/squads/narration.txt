One agent used to do everything, one task at a time.

Meet Squads in Zana.

Squads let a team of agents plan, split, and deliver work together.

There are two ways to build one. The quickest: just ask an agent.

It writes the squad definition straight into your project.

And it shows up in Settings, ready to launch.

Or build one yourself. Click New squad, and give it a name.

Add one slot per role: an orchestrator, two workers, and a reviewer.

Crown the orchestrator. It starts first, plans the work, and delegates.

Optionally, set an opening prompt, a project, worktrees, and launch approval.

Save it, and your squad joins the catalogue.

To launch, open New agent, choose Squad, and describe the goal.

The orchestrator breaks it into tasks, and workers pick them up in parallel.

Flow view shows who is working, and how work hands off.

When the squad needs a decision, it asks you in the Inbox.

Answer once, and the squad finishes the job.

Ask an agent, or build it yourself. Then launch it for any goal.
