# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Zana community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic
status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or
other participation that does not follow this Code of Conduct. Report concerns
privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/salesforce/zana/security/advisories/new).
