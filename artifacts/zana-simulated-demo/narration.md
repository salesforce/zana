# Zana walkthrough narration

**00:00:00** Meet Zana. A place to organize your projects, work with AI agents, and keep everything in view.

**00:00:07** Start by adding a project.

**00:00:11** Choose a local folder. You can also clone a repository or connect to a remote project.

**00:00:19** Now your work has a home.

**00:00:23** Create an agent and choose your preferred provider. Here, we'll use Codex.

**00:00:29** Describe the outcome you want, along with any constraints. Then send it off.

**00:00:36** The agent reads the project and starts making changes. You can follow its activity as it works.

**00:00:46** The Agents board gives you an overview across your projects.

**00:00:50** When a decision needs your input, the thread moves to Needs you.

**00:00:55** Open the question, choose an answer, and let the work continue.

**00:01:00** You can inspect the changed files right beside the conversation.

**00:01:05** Watch the checks finish, then review the result.

**00:01:10** The agent summarizes what changed. Open the preview to see the dashboard it built.

**00:01:19** Back on the board, the thread is idle and ready for your next instruction.

**00:01:25** With the GUS plugin connected to your account, your tickets are right here.

**00:01:30** Choose Me and the current sprint to focus on your own work.

**00:01:35** Open a ticket to see its details. Watch it to keep it close.

**00:01:41** The conversation and acceptance criteria stay with the ticket.

**00:01:47** Work with agent prepares a draft with the ticket context. Review it, then send.

**00:01:53** The agent can pick up from the work you're already tracking.

**00:01:59** Plugins bring your tools into Zana. And you can create your own.

**00:02:05** Describe your idea in plain language. Let's make a Focus Board with daily priorities, checkboxes, and a completion count.

**00:02:17** The agent uses the Plugin Guide, builds the panel, and checks its behavior.

**00:02:26** You can follow the build just like any other task.

**00:02:33** Review the plugin's access, then install and enable it locally.

**00:02:41** Your new tool appears in the sidebar, ready to use.

**00:02:46** Check off a priority and watch the count update.

**00:02:50** Add another task. A small tool, built around your day.

**00:02:59** Start a project. Give an agent a goal. Stay in the loop, keep your tickets close, and make Zana your own.
