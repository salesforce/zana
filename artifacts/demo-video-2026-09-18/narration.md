# Zana walkthrough narration

## 00:00:00 — Your work. One command center.

Meet Zana: one place to organize your projects, launch AI agents, and keep track of their work. Let's walk through a project, then explore GUS tickets and custom plugins.

## 00:00:12 — Start with a project

Start with the plus button beside Projects. Add a local folder, clone from Git, or connect a remote project.

## 00:00:20 — Give the work a home

Choose your folder and select Add project. Your project appears in the sidebar, giving your agents a clear place to work.

## 00:00:28 — Choose your agent

Open New agent from the project. Choose a Modern conversation or a CLI agent, then select your installed provider and model.

## 00:00:36 — Describe a concrete outcome

Give the agent a clear objective and constraints. Here, we ask for three milestones and an acceptance checklist for a fictional task dashboard. Select Send to start the work.

## 00:00:47 — The agent gets to work

The thread shows the agent working, with a place to send follow-ups or stop the task.

## 00:00:54 — See where your attention is needed

Open Agents for a view across your work, and filter by project or task. The board separates Needs you, Working, Idle, and Done, so you can spot what needs attention.

## 00:01:05 — Inspect the work, then steer it

Open a thread and expand its activity to inspect commands, file changes, and progress. You can add a follow-up whenever the task needs direction.

## 00:01:16 — Review the result

The agent has created the plan and summarized the result. Review the output before moving on to the next milestone.

## 00:01:24 — Keep your GUS work in view

With the GUS plugin configured for your account, My work brings your tickets into Zana. Choose a sprint and use the Me filter to focus on your own work, or inspect the team's board.

## 00:01:35 — Go from a ticket to an agent

Open a ticket to review its status, details, and chatter. Watch tickets you want to follow. Work with agent opens a draft with the ticket context, ready for your instructions.

## 00:01:47 — Make Zana your own

Plugins add tools to your command center. To build your own, open Plugins and choose New plugin.

## 00:01:54 — Describe the tool you want

Describe what you want in plain language. Our example is a Focus Board with three daily tasks, checkboxes, and a completion count.

## 00:02:03 — Build on the app's existing surfaces

The built-in Plugin Guide shows where a plugin can appear, including panels, project views, and thread tools. An agent can build and verify the plugin. Review it before installing it locally.

## 00:02:16 — Start a project. Give it a goal.

That's the workflow: organize the project, delegate the task, and monitor the result. Keep your tickets nearby, and shape Zana around the way you work.
