# Meet Zana

A 12-slide introduction to Zana, designed for people discovering the product.
The deck follows the Fairy identity: midnight blue, pearl white, blue and
lavender accents, and a golden spark.

- **Meet-Zana-Introduction.pdf** — ready to share and present.
- **Meet-Zana-Introduction.pptx** — editable titles, text, workflow diagrams,
  connectors, and links. Product captures and the architecture/plugin artwork
  are embedded images. Speaker notes contain source references.
- **images/** — four new explanatory PNGs, plus the complete architecture and
  plugin illustrations as PNGs and editable SVG masters.
